# **App Name**: CineEnroll

## Core Features:

- Interactive Registration Form: An interactive online registration form with a cinematic theme, fully responsive and user-friendly.
- Automated Data Collection: Collect and automatically store user data in the integrated database.
- Automated Email Notifications: Configure the tool to send confirmation emails to users upon successful registration.
- Dynamic Countdown Timer: Implement a countdown timer displaying the time remaining until November 20th, visually emphasizing the urgency of the deadline.
- Lightbox Photo Gallery: Create an automatically advancing carrousel that showcases pictures related to filmmaking with the possibility to click to open a picture in a Lightbox view.
- AI Form Assistant: Integrate Manus IA to generate personalized tips for filling the 'Expectations' open question to nudge the student in the right direction, acting as a tool to increase motivation.
- Unique Registration Number Generation: Generate a unique registration number (CIN-2025-XXX) upon successful submission and save it in the database.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Intense cinema red (#8B0000) for accents and CTAs, conveying excitement.
- Background color: Deep black (#000000) providing a dramatic backdrop.
- Accent color: Soft beige/off-white (#F5F5DC) for text, ensuring readability against the dark background.
- Headline font: 'Playfair' (serif) for titles to bring elegance, readability and impact.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body text to enhance readability.
- Employ movie-related icons (clapboard, film reel) to enrich section headers with a cinematic touch.
- Generous spacing to provide uncluttered layout and ease of navigation.
- Gentle fade-in animation to make the site more pleasant.
